Mohamed Ramadan Builds Secure, Scalable Backends with Java and Spring Boot

Two years in production, a platform serving more than 1,000 daily active users, and a multi-tenant SaaS backend shipped in 2026.

By the NewsroomCairo, EgyptBackend Developer

CAIRO — Mohamed Ramadan, a backend developer with two years of professional experience, builds secure, scalable and maintainable systems with Java and Spring Boot. He delivered a complete School Management System and supported a production platform serving more than 1,000 daily active users.

His toolbox centers on RESTful API development and Spring Security, including JWT, Keycloak and role-based access control, alongside Spring Data JPA, Hibernate, PostgreSQL, Redis and Docker. He is currently learning Apache Kafka.

A graduate of Ain Shams University's Bachelor of Computer Science programme (2017 to 2022), Ramadan has worked as a Java Backend Developer at Candles Company and KartNGo. At KartNGo he collaborated with a team of five or more developers to plan, implement, test and deliver backend features.

Most recently he built TaskFlow, a Jira-style, multi-tenant task management backend with workspace-level data isolation and five-tier role-based access control. Turn the page for the full record.
